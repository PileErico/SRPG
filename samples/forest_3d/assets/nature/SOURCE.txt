This folder contains 10 selected, unmodified GLB files from Models/GLTF format/.
The models use embedded geometry and material colors, without external textures.
NatureAssets.gd supplies runtime size / origin normalization and a muted palette.
Those sample-specific appearance changes do not modify the original files.
